To guarantee continuous event operations and protect sound, lighting, and stage equipment from voltage drops or venue power cuts, a dedicated generator is compulsory for all events.
  • Coverage: Exclusively powers our event equipment (it does not power venue lighting, hall fans, or air-conditioning).
  • Higher loads: LED walls, live band backline setups, or heavy lighting require higher-capacity generator units at additional cost.
  • Evening weddings: Additional generator fuel running charges apply.
