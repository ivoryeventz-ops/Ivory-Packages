  • Complete service supervision: coordination and supervision of all services booked through Ivory Events.
  • Final readiness check: confirming every arrangement is in place for the celebrations.
Wedding Day – Church Ceremony
  • Manthrakodi & ring box: arranged and handed over at the church entrance.
  • Bridal procession boards: elegant “Here Comes the Groom” and “Here Comes the Bride” boards.
  • Bridal entry assistance: guidance and coordination for a graceful entry into the church.
  • Garland & bouquet assistance: support with arrangements following the ceremony.
Wedding Day – Reception
  • Bridal party entry: guidance and coordination for the reception entrance.
  • Stage music coordination: dedicated music cues for
    • Bridal entry
    • Welcome drink
    • Candle lighting
    • Cake cutting
  • Two welcome hostesses: assisting with the welcome drink, candle lighting and cake cutting, and with guests during the photo session.
  • Stage photography assistance: coordination to keep the photography flowing smoothly.
A note on the welcome hostesses: they support the ceremonies and guests listed above, but they do not anchor or host the reception. If you would like a professional Anchor / MC to host, announce, engage with guests and run the reception programme, one can be arranged at an additional cost.
