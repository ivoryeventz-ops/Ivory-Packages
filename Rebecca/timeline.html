1. The Day Before & Morning Prep

The Day Before

Reception Venue Setup

Our team will reach the reception venue in the afternoon or evening before the wedding, depending on when the venue opens up, other activities taking place there, and the setup required.

  • Stage décor and other arrangements begin as soon as venue access is available.
  • Flowers, lighting, and final details are completed towards the end of the setup.
  • The entire venue will be ready at least 30 minutes before the reception begins.
Wedding Morning

Car Décor & Things to Keep Ready

On the morning of the wedding, the fresh-flower decoration for the groom’s car will be delivered to the groom’s residence.

The bride’s car, an Audi A6, will arrive at Cherys Bungalow Homestay by 9 AM with the car decoration already fixed on it.

Before leaving for church, please keep these items together and ready:

  • Manthrakodi
  • Minnu
  • Wedding Rings
  • Vaazhthumaala (gold chain)
Helpful Tip for the Manthrakodi & Minnu To stop the Manthrakodi from unfolding during the ceremony, tie its four corners lightly with a small thread. Traditionally, the Minnu is prepared using threads taken from the Manthrakodi, bonded with cooked rice. Many families now prefer a glue stick, which is cleaner and easier.

2. Reaching the Church & The Service

Timing

Arrive Early, Stay Relaxed

We recommend that the bride and groom reach the church at least 30 minutes before the service. This gives the photographers enough time for:

  • Arrival photographs
  • Wedding-car photographs
  • Couple portraits
  • Family photographs
  • A few relaxed moments before the service
No Need to Rush Inside There is no need to walk into the church immediately after arriving. Allow time for photographs and family coordination, and let the procession begin at a comfortable pace. It helps if the bride’s family follows a similar timeline, so that both the bride and groom are seated at least 5 minutes before the service begins.
Preparing for the Procession

Handing Over the Wedding Items

Once you reach the church, please make sure the following are ready:

Basket

Manthrakodi Basket

  1. Manthrakodi
  2. Minnu
  3. Vaazhthumaala
Box

Ring Bearer Box

  1. Bride’s wedding ring
  2. Groom’s wedding ring

The items may be carried by one person or shared between two people.

  • Please hand over the Manthrakodi basket and ring box to the sexton at least 5 minutes before the procession begins.
  • The sexton will place the items on the designated table near the altar.
  • Once the items are placed, our team will collect the basket and ring box and coordinate them for the procession.
Family Tradition Traditionally, the Manthrakodi is carried to the altar by a close female relative of the groom, such as a sister, cousin, or aunt.
Walking In

The Procession

The exact order can vary slightly from church to church. A typical MarThoma procession follows this order:

Groom’s Side

1. Groom’s Party

  1. Manthrakodi and wedding rings
  2. Groom with parents and family
  3. Groomsmen, if any
Bride’s Side

2. Bride’s Party

  1. Flower girls, if any
  2. Bride with parents
  3. Family members
  4. Bridesmaids

Bridesmaids and groomsmen may enter individually or in pairs, depending on your preference and the church’s practice.

Please Confirm With the Vicar Some priests prefer the bride to enter before the groom. It is always a good idea to confirm the preferred order with Achen in advance.
The Service

The Wedding Ceremony

The wedding service will proceed according to the church’s customs and schedule.

3. After the Service

Formalities

Signing the Register & Church Photographs

The service is usually followed by a few formalities:

  • Signing the register
  • Offertory
  • Photographs with the bishop and priests

Please allow approximately 30 to 45 minutes for these formalities.

At the Church Entrance

Garland & Bouquet Presentation

Once the church formalities are done, you will head to the church entrance for the welcome. The couple will receive:

  • 2 garlands
  • 2 greeting bouquets
Please Assign Four People in Advance Two people to present the garlands and two to present the bouquets. You may choose younger family members or anyone close to the couple.

To keep to the schedule, we recommend limiting photographs at the church to:

  • Clergy
  • Immediate family
  • Those presenting the garlands and bouquets at the church entrance

Everyone else can join you for photographs at the reception venue.

4. The Reception

Before You Arrive

Keep the Hall Doors Open

As guests begin arriving, we strongly recommend keeping the hall doors open so they can walk in, find their seats, and settle in without waiting outside.

Some caterers prefer to keep the doors closed until the couple arrives. Based on our experience, keeping them open makes for a more comfortable experience for guests. The final decision is, of course, entirely yours.

Getting In

Welcoming the Couple & Stage Entry

When you arrive at the venue, these family members may gather near the entrance to welcome you:

  • Parents
  • Siblings
  • Bridesmaids and groomsmen
  • Cousins and other close family members
Moving Into the Hall

Stage Entry, Made Quick

Once the anchor welcomes the couple, you may start moving into the hall.

To save time, it is a good idea to welcome the couple, parents and siblings onto the stage first, without an introduction. The family introduction can be done after the official functions on stage are over.

Stage Flow

The Stage Programme

The order of proceedings may be adjusted according to the priest’s preference. A typical order:

  • 1. Prayer & Blessing: The reception generally begins with a prayer and blessing for the newlyweds.
  • 2. Welcome Drink: The mothers of the bride and groom receive the glasses first and then present the drinks to the groom and bride. Fizzy apple juice is commonly used.
  • 3. Candle-Lighting: Two common traditions. Option 1: the couple lights all three candles together. Option 2: the parents light the two outer candles and the couple lights the centre candle.
  • 4. Cake Cutting: A two-tier wedding cake is placed on the cake stand for the cake-cutting ceremony.
  • 5. Introduction: The MC will introduce the newlyweds, their parents, and their families.

The order of candle lighting and cake cutting may be changed according to the priest’s preference.

Our hostesses will be on stage throughout to assist with the drinks, lighter, and cake knife, and to guide everyone smoothly through each of these moments.

A Quick Anchor Tip If you are bringing your own MC or anchor, let the priest know beforehand so they can agree on whether the prayer happens at the start or the end of the stage program.
During the Meal

Manthrakodi Dress Change

While guests are having their meal, the dress change into the Manthrakodi will take place in the green room of the hall. Please:

  • Keep the outfit easily accessible.
  • Make sure the saree drapist is at the hall at the scheduled time.
  • Allow enough time to return without delaying photography.
Stage Photography

Photographs With Family & Guests

Once you are back from the dress change, stage photography begins.

Our hostess will guide guests to come up on one side of the stage and exit through the other end. Once stage photography is complete, you can finally sit down, relax, and enjoy a meal together with your close family.

Departure

Leaving for the Kacha Ceremony

At the end of the reception, the newlyweds will leave for the groom’s home for the traditional Kacha ceremony.

A Little Note on Gifts & Blessings

On your big day, so many loved ones will come up to the stage to bless you, bringing gifts, envelopes, jewellery, ornaments, and bouquets with all their love.

Our team will be right beside you, taking care of the stage and keeping everything flowing beautifully. We do ask for your understanding that we are unable to take charge of gifts, valuables, or personal belongings, so that we can give our full attention to your programme.

What we gently suggest: ask someone you trust from the family to stand beside the stage and:

  • Collect gifts and envelopes as they come.
  • Receive jewellery and other valuables.
  • Keep a simple note of what has been received.
  • Hand everything safely over to the family once the function is done.

If needed, items can be kept for a short while behind the sofa or in another spot near the stage, at the family’s own risk. Ivory Events cannot be held responsible for the loss, damage, or misplacement of gifts, valuables, envelopes, jewellery, ornaments, bouquets, or other belongings before, during, or after the event.

We’re here to help you enjoy every minute

A wedding day can feel busy, but it does not have to be stressful. A little preparation in advance, especially around timings, wedding items, the family members involved in ceremonies, photography, and transportation, helps everything run smoothly.

Our team will coordinate the arrangements and guide you through the day wherever needed. Your only job is to enjoy the day, spend time with your family and friends, and celebrate this beautiful beginning together.
